Ordering

Is the €250 deposit refundable?

Yes, fully, at any time until your bike ships. Cancel from your account or email us and the deposit is returned to your original payment method within 5 working days.

When will my bike arrive?

The first production run of 1,200 bikes ships from April 2027 in reservation order. We'll invite you to confirm your final configuration in February 2027 and give you a delivery week then.

Can I change my configuration after reserving?

Yes. Your reservation holds your place in the queue, not a fixed spec. Change colour, wheels, battery or accessories freely until you confirm in February 2027.

Riding

How fast does the Volta One go?

Motor assistance stops at 25 km/h, the legal limit for e-bikes in the EU, so no licence, insurance or helmet law applies beyond what covers a regular bike. You can pedal faster without assistance.

How heavy is it, and can I carry it upstairs?

The Volta One weighs 18.9 kg ready to ride, light for a 720 Wh e-bike. The top tube is shaped as a carry handle, and the battery comes out in one motion if you need to shave off 3.6 kg.

Battery

How long does charging take?

0–80 % takes about 2.5 hours on the included 4 A charger (3 hours for the 960 Wh battery). A full charge takes about 4 hours. Charge on or off the bike.

How long will the battery last?

The cells keep at least 80 % of their capacity after 1,000 full cycles, roughly 100,000 km for most riders. The battery is covered by the 2-year warranty and replacements are sold separately.

Service

What maintenance does it need?

Very little. The carbon belt needs no oil and the stepless hub is sealed. We recommend a check every 5,000 km or once a year; our mobile mechanics come to you in nine cities.

What does the warranty cover?

Two years on all components including motor and battery, and five years on the frame and fork. Wear parts like tyres, brake pads and the belt are not covered after normal wear.

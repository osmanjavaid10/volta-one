Every part, on show

Engineered in the open.

  1. 01 — Profile

    One line, front to back.

    A single sweep from head tube to rear axle. No bolted-on battery, no cable spaghetti, nothing that rattles.

  2. 02 — Exploded

    Six parts. Zero compromises.

    We designed every component in-house so they fit like they were cut from one block — because, mostly, they were.

  3. 03 — Mid-drive

    85 Nm at 23 dB.

    Torque sensing reads your pedal stroke 1,000 times a second. Hills flatten. The motor stays quieter than the wind.

    85Nm

  4. 04 — Battery

    720 Wh, hidden in plain sight.

    Sealed into the down tube, out in one motion with the key. 0–80 % in two and a half hours from any socket.

    720Wh

  5. 05 — Drivetrain

    A belt that outlasts the chain.

    Gates carbon belt and a stepless hub. No oil, no grease on your trousers, 30,000 km between services.

    0oil

  6. 06 — Cockpit

    Only what you need.

    A 120 lux light bar and a two-inch display: speed, range, time. Everything else lives in the app, out of your way.

    120lux

  7. 07 — Together

    Back in one piece. Yours in April.

    18.9 kg, ready to ride. Assembled and tested by hand in Amsterdam before it leaves the workshop.

Design details

Small decisions, made obsessively.

  • 0visible cables

    Routed inside

    Brake, light and motor lines run inside the frame and bar. The cockpit looks like one piece because it is.

  • IP67

    Rain-proof, really

    Motor, battery and connectors are sealed to IP67. Dutch weather tested it more thoroughly than our lab could.

  • 203mm discs

    Stops that feel inevitable

    Four-piston hydraulic brakes with oversized rotors. The bike slows down exactly as much as you ask.

  • 2year warranty

    Backed properly

    Two years on everything, five on the frame, and a mobile mechanic in nine cities who comes to you.
